ingestion
ETL for everyday life

Casual conversation
into structured data.

Text like you normally would — “ran 5km this morning”, a photo of dinner. ingestion structures it into typed, reviewed rows in the databases your apps already own. If details are missing, it queues the item — or lazily researches your other sources to complete it.

  • one contract file per app
  • no glue code
  • local-first
